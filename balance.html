ABOUT THE BALANCE
⚖ ⚖

Each tile is a two-digit number. Flip a tile to reverse its digits. When both digit columns total the same amount, the Balance is complete.

The first number is sealed and cannot be changed.

You may ask the apparatus to weigh your arrangement three times. A failed weighing is not returned.
